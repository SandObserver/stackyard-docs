## What it shows

Each row shows the day and time on the left, and the title over the episode, release or artist. A coloured bar marks the kind: episode, film or album. The widget reads the next 14 days. With fewer than eight items, it reads the next 90 days.

- **Today:** today's items say Today, in red.
- **Episodes:** a row shows the season and episode, and marks a premiere or a finale. Medium and large also show the episode name, unless the row is a premiere or a finale. Episodes of one series at the same time share a row.
- **Films:** each release date you choose is its own row.
- **Albums:** a row shows the artist and the album type.
- **Ended:** an episode leaves the list when it ends. A film or album leaves at the end of its day.

Clicking a row opens the series, film or album in its app. With nothing to list, the card opens the app's calendar.

You can add a second Sonarr or Radarr, for example for 4K. An item two apps both list shows once. If one app fails, the others still show, and a line names the app that failed.

The widget only reads.
