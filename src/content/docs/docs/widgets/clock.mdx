A digital, analog or Captain clock. It runs in the browser and needs no service. The digital and analog faces follow [Display Mode](/docs/customization/).

The Captain face is a comic panel: a speech balloon with the weekday, time and date, and a glass of beer whose level changes each minute. Its wall colour follows the time of day.
