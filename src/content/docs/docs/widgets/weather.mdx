## UV and air quality warnings

An icon appears in the top corner when the UV index is 3 or higher, or when the air is unhealthy. Hover or focus the card, or tap the icon, to see the reading. Escape hides it. When both warn, the icon shows the worse one. Screen readers hear both.

<WidgetGroup caption="Very high UV. Hover the card to see the reading."
  items={[
    { type: 'weather', file: 'index.html', id: 'w-weather-uv', title: 'Weather, UV warning', label: 'UV 8' },
  ]} />

| Reading | Warns from |
| --- | --- |
| UV index | 3 |
| European Air Quality Index | 60 (Poor) |
| US Air Quality Index | 101 (Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups) |

Places in EU and EEA countries, the EU outermost regions, Åland, the United Kingdom and Switzerland use the European index. All other places use the US index.

Both readings come from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API, for either weather service. Air quality data is from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS).
