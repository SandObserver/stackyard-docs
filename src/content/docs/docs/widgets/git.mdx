Shows your open pull requests or your contribution graph. Pick one with the **View** setting.

<WidgetGroup caption="The pull requests shown are made up."

Both views come in every size. Point at a day on the contribution graph to see its date and count. The card follows the light and dark theme.

Pull requests are listed by last update.

`Key rejected` on the widget means the token is wrong, expired or missing a scope.
